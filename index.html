WHO I AM

I was raised in Madrid, with a Danish dad and a Peruvian mom, in an American household. My multicultural perspective might have influenced my interest in the importance of difference in the process of how groups think.

I moved to California, chasing surf, sailing, and I guess the water writ large. Outside of research, I am very much into bodybuilding, long-distance running, and increasingly gymnastics and calisthenics.

RESEARCH & ACADEMIA

I am currently a PhD student in Information Studies at UCLA, where my research centers on AI, swarm intelligence, collective intelligence, and multi-agent systems. I examine how digital protocols, information architectures, and computational systems shape group decision-making and coordination.

My doctoral committee is chaired by Ramesh Srinivasan (UCLA Information Studies), alongside Geoffrey Bowker (UC Irvine), Jonathan Furner (UCLA Information Studies), Peter John Lamberson (UCLA Communication), and Moritz Meyer-ter-Vehn (UCLA Economics).

My work is conducted with ongoing gratitude for the guidance and research funding provided by the UC Berkeley Center for Responsible Decentralized Intelligence (RDI) and the Center for Digital Assets (CDA).
